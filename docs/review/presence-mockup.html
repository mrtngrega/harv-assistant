HIS EYES

They follow the moon he's working with as it moves along its orbit, so you can tell what he's doing without reading. They blink when he's calm, scan while he thinks, turn amber and look down at your prompt when he needs a yes, become crosshairs on the desktop, close at a plan limit, and flinch on an error.

ONLY WHILE WORKING

At rest nothing hangs from the bar: only a faint glint on its edge. A turn starts and the drop falls into view in about half a second. When he's done there's a short flash, then he pulls back up two seconds later.

THE PILL

Outside the app he's a dot at the top of your screen; while he works it opens with his face, what he's doing and the six moons. It is also the desktop-control pill: with his crosshair eyes, STOP and the glow round the monitor. Mouse near pulls it up, except while he drives the desktop, so STOP stays reachable.
