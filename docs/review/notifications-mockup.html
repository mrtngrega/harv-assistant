Notifications

Kept per computer.

THREE LEVELS

Normal (a reply in another chat, a scheduled or background task done, a project synced): tick, fades after about 6 s.

Warning (a held yes, a question or plan waiting, usage at 75/90%, a busy program, an error, a sync conflict): chime, amber, stays until clicked.

Urgent (a threat, protection off, overheating, plan limit reached): chime, stronger amber, stays, plus a Windows notification and a phone push after 10 minutes away.

SEVERAL AT ONCE

One bubble, one line. The rest wait behind it, urgent first, with "+2" on the bubble. The same thing again is merged into its line ("replied ×3") and makes no second sound. If Harv is already working, the bubble hangs from the face that's already there.

RECENT

Today's notices, newest first, behind the [HARV] logo and behind the pill's face. What arrived while you were out of the app is marked, with the count as the top line. The phone's pairing code stays a card: it's a code to read and type.
